# Quote

A real person's words, verbatim, under a hairline rule with a large opening quote mark in the accent color, and who they are in muted text beneath.

## When to use
Inside a Role Panel, or beside teaching or work history where a colleague's or student's words back up a claim. At most one quote per panel or section.

## Markup
`figure.wm-quote > blockquote > p` (the words) + `figcaption` (who said it). The caption gets its "— " from the stylesheet; don't type it.

## Color
The quote mark uses `--wm-accent`: violet inside the product Role Panel, `gold` everywhere else.

## Content rules
- Only real quotes, with the person's permission, copied word for word. To shorten, use one or more whole sentences rather than cutting inside one, and keep the person's own capitals and punctuation.
- Attribute by relationship and place, not by name: "Former co-worker at Ultimate Software (UKG)", "Former student at the University of Phoenix".
- One to three sentences; around 20 to 25 words fits a panel in three lines on desktop.

## Don't
Invent, paraphrase or combine quotes, or style a quote in `script`.
